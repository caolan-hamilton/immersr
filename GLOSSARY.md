# Crosstalk

A language-immersion app: a live AI voice partner speaks only the language you're acquiring, you reply in English, and you build listening time through conversation rather than deliberate study.

## Language

**Crosstalk**:
The core technique: a spoken conversation where the Partner speaks only the Target language and the Learner is free to reply in English.
_Avoid_: Immersion mode, bilingual chat

**Learner**:
The person using the app to acquire a Target language.
_Avoid_: User, student, account

**Partner**:
The AI voice the Learner converses with. It understands English and the Target language but only ever speaks the Target language.
_Avoid_: Bot, tutor, agent, assistant

**Target language**:
The language the Learner is acquiring and the Partner speaks.
_Avoid_: Foreign language, L2

**Level**:
The Learner's self-chosen CEFR band (A1, A2, B1 or B2) for a Session, which sets how simply the Partner speaks.
_Avoid_: Difficulty, proficiency, skill

**Session**:
One continuous live voice conversation between a Learner and the Partner, in one Target language, at one Level, about one Topic.
_Avoid_: Call, chat, lesson

**Topic**:
A short free-text subject the Learner chooses for a Session, which the Partner talks about.
_Avoid_: Scenario, prompt, theme

**Daily allowance**:
The maximum Session time a Learner may use per day, across all Target languages.
_Avoid_: Quota, credits, limit

**Reservation**:
The maximum Session length held against a Learner's Daily allowance when a Session starts; whatever goes unused is returned when the Session ends.
_Avoid_: Hold, lock, pre-charge

**Immersion time**:
The total time the Partner has spent speaking to a Learner in a Target language, accumulated across Sessions; the Learner's measure of progress.
_Avoid_: Study time, minutes, hours, streak

**Turn**:
One uninterrupted stretch of speech by either the Learner or the Partner within a Session.
_Avoid_: Message, utterance

**Rephrase**:
A Partner Turn that restates something the Learner didn't understand more simply, still in the Target language.
_Avoid_: Fallback, translation, hint

**Transcript**:
The live written record of a Session's Turns, shown to the Learner as the conversation happens.
_Avoid_: Log, captions, history

**Turn translation**:
The English rendering of a single Partner Turn, shown when the Learner taps it in the Transcript.
_Avoid_: Tap-to-translate, gloss
